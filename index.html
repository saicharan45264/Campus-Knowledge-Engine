01 — Objectives

Project Objectives

Core engineering and research targets formulated for the M.A.C.H. AI cognitive platform.

01

Hierarchical Neo4j Curriculum Knowledge Graph

Construct a typed Neo4j curriculum knowledge graph modeling the complete academic hierarchy: Department → Course → Unit → Topic → Question. Ground all graph entities in statutory source documents (Curriculum Syllabi and Past Year Question papers / PYQs), linking multi-hop prerequisite dependencies ([:REQUIRES*1..5]) and Course Outcomes to guarantee 100% academic provenance.

02

Student-Centric Context-Grounded Query Answering

Deliver accurate, hallucination-free answers tailored specifically to student inquiries. Structured questions regarding syllabi, prerequisites, and PYQs are resolved deterministically into interactive cards with marks and Bloom levels, while conceptual queries are synthesized via PostgreSQL hybrid RRF search grounded strictly in verified curriculum text.

03

Multimodal Diagram Grounding & Deterministic ID

Preserve visual examination assets (diagrams, equations, and vector drawings) through PDF coordinate slicing, vector path expansion (page.get_drawings), and cross-page stitching at 2x resolution. Assign 100% idempotent MD5 question identities and bind technical figures directly to assessment nodes and Bloom's Taxonomy Levels.

04

Two-Stage Constrained Topic Mapping & Curriculum Alignment

Implement a two-stage retriever-ranker with selective abstention that automatically maps raw examination questions to canonical syllabus topics with 99.3% precision, eliminating hallucinated topic tagging and linking every assessment problem to accredited Course Outcomes (CO) and Bloom's Taxonomy Levels (BTL).

02 — Problem Statement

The Problem We Are Solving

Universities manage large curriculum documents and past-year question papers that are difficult to organize and access efficiently. Traditional systems struggle to retrieve accurate, course-specific information with low latency. M.A.C.H. addresses these challenges through graph-based knowledge representation, hybrid retrieval, and layout-preserving question paper extraction.
Student Academic Discovery

Information Fragmentation & Exam Prep Friction

  • Students spend excessive time manually searching across disparate multi-semester PDF handbooks for course details and syllabus topics.
  • Finding relevant past-year exam questions along with their diagrams and equations is difficult and disorganized.
  • M.A.C.H. Solution: Single cognitive query interface that delivers verified syllabus pathways, prerequisite sequences, and diagram-grounded past exam questions instantly.
Challenge 01

Preserving Multi-Hop Prerequisites

Chunking institutional handbooks into isolated vector chunks breaks relational edges across 4 years of study, causing traditional search to miss mandatory foundational prerequisite sequences.

Challenge 02

Retaining Diagrams

Text-only OCR pipelines silently discard raster and vector graphic streams, completely decoupling technical examination questions from the diagrams essential for answering them.

Challenge 03

Accurate Course-Specific Retrieval

Conventional vector search produces cross-course false positives when different subjects share common concepts; M.A.C.H. leverages graph topology and hybrid RRF search to deliver verified, course-grounded answers with minimal latency.

03 — Methodology

System Architecture & Methodology

A five-stage asymmetric engineering framework designed for Vision-Language Intelligent Learning (VLIL).

01

Boundary-Preserving Parsing

High-throughput deterministic regex state machine parses 350-page curriculum handbooks in under 8 seconds, with selective LLM verification for ambiguous units.

PyMuPDFRegex FSMZero-Hallucination
02

Spatial Diagram Slicing

Spatial coordinate scanning and vector path expansion crop figures from past examination papers, stitching multi-page drawings and generating deterministic MD5 hashes.

Bounding Box2x RenderPillow Stitching
03

Curriculum Knowledge Graph

Constructs a typed Neo4j property graph modeling courses, topics, units, prerequisite chains (depth 1–5), Course Outcomes (CO), and Bloom's Taxonomy Levels.

Neo4j 5.24CypherDAG Topology
04

Dual-Channel Hybrid Search

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF, k=60) merges BM25/tsvector lexical rankings with dense nomic-embed-text vectors in PostgreSQL (pgvector HNSW) for theory search.

PostgreSQL 16pgvectorRRF Ranking
05

Fast-Path & Gemma 4 12B

Two-tier intent routing serves deterministic queries in 80–120ms; local on-premise Gemma 4 12B streams grounded synthesis under strict campus data privacy.
